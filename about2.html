01 / LEARNING BY DOING

Experience in practice.

From public infrastructure to web-based engineering applications. Open a role to explore the work.

University of Wisconsin–Madison

Building and maintaining the engineering tools industry partners rely on.

  • Application development. Develop and maintain web-based engineering applications on a Linux, Apache, MySQL, and PHP (LAMP) stack.
  • Modernization & usability. Modernized the Charge Management Tool by removing redundant code and migrating to JSON storage while preserving compatibility with existing project files. Added equipment duplication to the Safety Relief Vent Tool to reduce repetitive data entry.
  • Security & reliability. Remediated a vulnerability that could have enabled session theft. Restored critical website and database services from backups following an accidental deletion.
  • Better development workflows. Introduced Git-based version control to improve change tracking and maintainability across multiple applications.

Germantown, Wisconsin

Taking engineering from the office into the field.

  • Surveying & inspections. Operated GPS land surveying equipment to verify record drawings and performed routine erosion control inspections.
  • Team & records. Trained new team members in erosion control methods and inspection. Organized and digitized historical engineering records to improve departmental efficiency.

Mequon, Wisconsin

Connecting field data with the infrastructure beneath our feet.

  • Mapping & fieldwork. Used Esri ArcGIS to map and update underground utility data. Operated surveying GPS equipment and assisted with field data collection.
  • Construction oversight. Conducted road construction inspections and routine erosion control checks.

White Lake, Wisconsin

Helping young campers learn, explore, and solve problems.

  • Teaching & service. Taught first aid, camping, and wilderness survival to elementary school-aged children. Worked with adults to resolve campsite cleanliness issues through problem solving and conflict resolution.
